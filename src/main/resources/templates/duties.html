The ladder in force

Configured, not institutional

These ranks and shares come from this installation's configuration. They are a starting point this application chose, not a policy of any institution — an examination office should confirm them and change them in edsa.duties.ranks.

Weighting by rank is on; a course teacher may not invigilate their own paper; the minimum per invigilator is 1 and the cap is none. A seniority value on a faculty record overrides its designation.
